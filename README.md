# Task 4 – Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming structured sales data into meaningful visualizations and a clear business story using Microsoft Power BI.

## Business Question

What factors are driving sales and profit performance?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Superstore Sales Dataset

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Business Overview
Provides an overview of total sales, total profit, quantity, and overall sales and profit trends.

### 2. Sales & Profit Trend
Shows changes in sales and profit over time and compares performance across product categories.

### 3. Category & Product Performance
Analyzes sales and profit by category and highlights top-performing products.

### 4. Regional Performance
Compares sales and profit across regions and states.

### 5. Key Insights & Recommendations
Summarizes the major business insights obtained from the visual analysis.

## Key Insights

- Sales and profit performance varies across time periods.
- Product categories contribute differently to overall sales and profitability.
- Individual products have different levels of sales and profit contribution.
- Regional and state-level performance varies across the business.

## Deliverable

A 5-page Power BI visual story communicating business trends, patterns, and insights.
